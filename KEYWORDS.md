# Roots Store Keyword Strategy

## Apple App Store keyword field

Recommended US English field (99 characters):

`diet,food scanner,allergy,vegan,jain,halal,kosher,gluten free,barcode,ingredients,vegetarian,recipe`

Do not repeat “Roots,” words already carrying strong weight in the title/subtitle, plural variants, or unrelated medical claims. Localize keywords by market rather than translating this field mechanically.

## Apple secondary concepts

Use naturally in the description and localization:

- restaurant finder
- food safety
- dietary profile
- meal planning
- ingredient label
- cross-contact
- dairy free
- egg free

“Food safety” should appear only in the context of evidence and confirmation—not as a guarantee.

## Google Play strategy

Google Play does not use a separate keyword field. Use search terms naturally in the short and full descriptions:

- diet and dietary profile
- food scanner and barcode scanner
- ingredients and ingredient label
- restaurant finder
- allergy and cross-contact
- vegan and vegetarian
- Jain, Halal, and Kosher
- gluten free
- meal planning and Meal Builder
- recipe modification
- food safety

Avoid repeating lists solely for ranking. Measure listing performance after launch without adding analytics to the app.
