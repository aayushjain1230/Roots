## App name

Roots

## Short description

Scan food, understand ingredients, and find choices that fit your dietary profile.

## Full description

Roots helps answer one practical question: “Can I eat this?”

Build a dietary profile, scan packaged foods, understand ingredient evidence, and explore restaurant options without turning uncertainty into a promise of safety.

PRODUCT SCANNING

• Scan packaged-food barcodes  
• Photograph ingredient labels  
• Review clear Safe, Caution, and Avoid guidance  
• Understand which ingredients affected the result  
• Save useful reports for later

PERSONALIZED GUIDANCE

Configure religious diets, lifestyle diets, allergies, cross-contact preferences, and custom restrictions. Roots applies deterministic dietary rules to the evidence available for a product or dish.

RESTAURANTS AND MEALS

Search by location and meal intent, review available menu evidence, compare dishes, and build an order using supported menu options. Roots highlights unknown ingredients, preparation questions, and cross-contact concerns instead of guessing.

FOOD ASSISTANT

Ask food questions, adapt a recipe, or request meal ideas. AI features explain or transform the information you provide. They never replace deterministic compatibility decisions.

TRAVEL MODE

Prepare dining cards and restaurant questions, translate supported phrases, and use device text-to-speech. Saved travel information and core local data remain available offline where supported.

LOCAL-FIRST PRIVACY

Dietary profiles, history, saved products, saved meals, preferences, and recommendations are stored locally. Roots has no ads or behavioral tracking. Network-backed actions transmit only the data needed to complete the request.

Roots is an informational tool, not medical advice. Ingredient lists and restaurant preparation can change. Confirm severe allergies, medical dietary needs, and uncertain preparation directly with the manufacturer or restaurant.
