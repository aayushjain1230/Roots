# Roots FAQ

## What does Roots do?

Roots helps evaluate packaged foods and restaurant dishes against a dietary profile. It combines barcode data, label/menu extraction, and deterministic dietary rules.

## Does “Safe” guarantee a food is safe for me?

No. A result reflects the evidence available to Roots. Ingredients, recipes, facilities, and restaurant preparation can change. Confirm severe allergies, medical needs, certifications, and uncertain preparation directly with the manufacturer or restaurant.

## Does AI decide whether I can eat something?

No. Compatibility verdicts come from deterministic rules. AI may extract text, translate, explain supplied evidence, adapt a recipe, or suggest meal ideas.

## Why does a result say “Needs Confirmation” or “Caution”?

The available evidence may omit an ingredient, sauce, broth, preparation method, certification, or cross-contact detail. Open the explanation to see what remains unknown.

## Why did barcode scanning return no ingredients?

The product database may not contain a complete ingredient list. Scan the physical ingredient label, check the packaging, or confirm with the manufacturer.

## Why did label or menu scanning fail?

Use bright, even lighting; avoid glare; keep text flat; include the full label; and ensure the image is sharp. OCR requires an internet connection and the Roots service to be available.

## Why does restaurant search need location?

Roots uses location only after you tap “Use My Location.” You can enter a location manually instead.

## What works offline?

The app shell, profile, history, Saved reports, shopping data, cached barcode products, and previously prepared travel information work offline where available. New barcode lookups, OCR, translations, restaurant searches, and AI tools require internet.

## Where is my data stored?

Most profile, history, saved, restaurant-memory, and travel data is stored locally on the device. Network-backed actions transmit only the data required for the requested task.

## How do I remove saved data?

Use the relevant deletion controls in Settings and Saved. Clearing browser/app storage or uninstalling the app may also remove local data. Back up anything important before doing so.

## Can Roots check Jain, Halal, Kosher, vegan, vegetarian, and allergy needs?

Roots supports these profile categories with different levels of available evidence. Certification and preparation details may still require confirmation. Review the explanation rather than relying on a label alone.
