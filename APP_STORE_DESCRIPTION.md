## Name

Roots

## Subtitle

Food guidance for your diet

## Promotional text

Scan products, understand ingredients, and explore restaurant options using one dietary profile. Roots explains what it knows—and clearly flags what still needs confirmation.

## Short description

Roots helps you answer “Can I eat this?” with barcode and label scanning, personalized dietary checks, restaurant menu analysis, and clear evidence-backed explanations.

## Long description

Roots is a dietary assistant built to make everyday food decisions clearer.

Create a profile for the dietary rules that matter to you, then use Roots while shopping, cooking, dining out, or traveling. Scan a barcode or ingredient label to review a product. Explore restaurants and menus to find dishes that fit your profile. Save useful products and meals for later.

CLEAR PRODUCT CHECKS

• Scan packaged-food barcodes  
• Photograph ingredient labels  
• Review ingredient-level reasons  
• See clear Safe, Caution, or Avoid guidance  
• Keep useful reports available in Saved

PERSONALIZED DIETARY PROFILES

Roots supports configurable religious and lifestyle diets, allergies, cross-contact preferences, and custom restrictions. Results come from deterministic dietary rules rather than an AI guess.

DINING OUT

Search for nearby restaurants when you choose to share a location. Import available menu information, review dish evidence, compare choices, and build a meal from options the restaurant actually lists. When information is missing, Roots tells you what needs confirmation.

ASK AND UNDERSTAND

Use the assistant to ask food questions, adapt recipes, or explore meal ideas. AI can explain supplied evidence, but it does not override Roots’ deterministic compatibility results.

TRAVEL WITH CLEARER COMMUNICATION

Prepare dietary cards and restaurant questions, translate supported content, and use device text-to-speech when speaking with restaurant staff. Previously prepared travel information can remain available offline.

PRIVACY-FIRST BY DESIGN

Profiles, history, saved products, saved meals, preferences, and many recommendations remain on your device. Network-backed features send only the information needed for the action you request. Roots contains no advertising or behavioral tracking.

IMPORTANT

Roots is an informational dietary assistant. Restaurant preparation, recipes, manufacturing processes, and ingredient sources can change. For severe allergies, medical dietary needs, or uncertain preparation, confirm directly with the manufacturer or restaurant.

## App Review notes

- No account or login is required.
- Camera access is requested only when the user starts a camera-based scan.
- Location access is requested only after the user selects “Use My Location” in restaurant search.
- Label/menu OCR, translation, and AI tools require the protected Roots backend.
- Dietary verdicts are deterministic; AI responses are explanatory only.
